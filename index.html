Multivariate Decoding of Theory of Mind Networks Using HCP fMRI Data

  • Investigated neural correlates of Theory of Mind using Human Connectome Project fMRI data from 100 subjects and Frith-Happé animations, where participants watched short animations of geometric shapes and judged whether they were interacting socially.
  • Modeled trial-level brain activity across 360 regions using a generalized linear model with hemodynamic response function, isolating per-region activation estimates for the 100 subjects.
  • Applied multivariate random forest decoding across the 360 regions, achieving 85% accuracy in classifying mental interaction vs. random motion.
  • Findings suggest that the joint, multivariate activity of mentalization regions together encodes information relevant to mentalization, corroborating known Theory of Mind regions: temporoparietal junction (TPJ), medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC), and inferior and superior temporal sulcus (STS).
